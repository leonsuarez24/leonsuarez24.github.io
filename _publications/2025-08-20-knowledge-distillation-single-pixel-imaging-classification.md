Single-pixel imaging acquires a scene through a sequence of scalar measurements taken with structured illumination patterns. Reconstructing an image before classifying it is wasteful when the task is only recognition.

This work distills a classifier trained on full-resolution images into a network that operates directly on the compressive single-pixel measurements, and jointly learns the sensing patterns. The student recovers a large part of the teacher's accuracy at a small fraction of the measurement budget.

Presented at STSIVA 2025.
