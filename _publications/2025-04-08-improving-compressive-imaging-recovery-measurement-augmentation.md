Data augmentation for learned reconstruction is usually performed in the image domain, which breaks the consistency between an image and its measurements.

This paper augments in the **measurement** domain instead: transformations are applied to the acquired data in a way that remains compatible with the sensing operator, producing additional valid measurement–image pairs. The augmented set regularizes training and improves recovery quality without any change to the optics.

Presented at ICASSP 2025 in Hyderabad.
