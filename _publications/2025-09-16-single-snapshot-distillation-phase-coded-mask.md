Phase retrieval recovers phase information from magnitude-only coded diffraction patterns. Reliable recovery normally demands several snapshots with different coded masks, which is expensive in acquisition time and hardware.

This paper applies knowledge distillation across the snapshot budget: a teacher system that captures multiple coded diffraction patterns supervises a student restricted to a **single** snapshot. The distillation objective drives the design of the student's phase coded mask so that one acquisition carries as much of the teacher's information as possible.

Presented at ICIP 2025 in Anchorage.
