A conditional diffusion model is trained to sample images consistent with compressive measurements. The conditioning signal is built from **augmented** measurements, which enriches the information available to the denoising network at each reverse step and reduces the ambiguity that comes from heavy undersampling.

The combination of a learned generative prior with measurement augmentation yields reconstructions that are both sharper and more data-consistent than the corresponding non-augmented conditional model.

Presented at ICASSP 2025 in Hyderabad.
