Gravimetry images subsurface density contrasts associated with geological structures, geothermal systems and intrusive bodies. The inversion is notoriously non-unique, and it suffers from a well-known depth bias: without an explicit depth weighting, recovered density anomalies collapse towards the surface.

This work represents the subsurface density field with an implicit neural representation — a coordinate network that maps 3D position to density — and equips it with a **depth-aware** prior, so that the continuous representation itself carries the depth sensitivity that the gravity operator lacks.

Part of the gravimetry line of work on the Cerro Machín volcano field data.
