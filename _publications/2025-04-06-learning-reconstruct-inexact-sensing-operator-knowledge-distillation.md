In real optical systems the sensing operator used at reconstruction time is never exactly the one implemented in hardware — misalignment, calibration error and manufacturing tolerances all introduce mismatch, and reconstruction networks are sensitive to it.

This paper treats the mismatch as a distillation gap. A teacher network reconstructs using the exact operator; a student that only has access to the inexact operator is trained to match the teacher's outputs and features. The student therefore learns to absorb the operator error rather than propagate it into the image.

Presented at ICASSP 2025 in Hyderabad.
