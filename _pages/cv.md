Education
======
* **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Universidad Industrial de Santander, Bucaramanga, Colombia — *in progress*
* **M.Sc. in Computer and Systems Engineering**, Universidad Industrial de Santander, Bucaramanga, Colombia — September 2025
* **B.Sc. in Civil Engineering**, *Cum Laude*, Universidad Industrial de Santander, Bucaramanga, Colombia — April 2023

Experience
======

* **January 2026 – July 2026** — *Assistant Researcher, Project 8091*, Universidad Industrial de Santander
  * Integrated deep-learning interpolation algorithms into a maintained shared repository.
  * Led technical meetings defining the computational requirements, development languages and graphical interface for the software integrating the developed algorithms.
  * Consolidated the final technical reports for Objective 2 of Project 8091 (O2R1, O2R2, O2R3).
  * Produced the final technical report validating the deep-learning model for interpolation of point geophysical measurements across sampling rates and topographic conditions.
  * Prepared a publishable manuscript incorporating the results of Objective 2.
  * Organized and delivered knowledge-appropriation activities: scientific events, workshops, seminars and knowledge-transfer strategies.

* **September 2025 – December 2025** — *Assistant Researcher, Project 8091*, Universidad Industrial de Santander
  * Reviewed the state of the art on interpolation strategies using coordinate-based and continuous-representation neural networks.
  * Formulated the spatial data interpolation problem with neural networks in mathematical terms.
  * Implemented and benchmarked several neural network models to validate their interpolation capability.
  * Validated interpolation results against gravimetry field data from the **Cerro Machín volcano**.
  * Authored the gravimetry interpolation chapter of reports O2R4 and O2R5, and presented Project 8091 results at scientific events.

* **August 2023 – December 2023** — *Assistant Researcher, Project 3925*, Universidad Industrial de Santander
  * Surveyed state-of-the-art techniques for problems in poststack seismic imaging.
  * Mathematically modelled the degradation of seismic images using image and signal processing techniques.
  * Implemented controlled degradation of poststack seismic images — noise, blur, geometric transformations and others.
  * Participated in the organization of social appropriation of knowledge and dissemination activities.

* **June 2023 – July 2023** — *Assistant Researcher, Project 3735*, Universidad Industrial de Santander
  * Designed and implemented a preliminary deep learning model for action recognition.
  * Carried out preliminary training of the deep learning architecture for action recognition.
